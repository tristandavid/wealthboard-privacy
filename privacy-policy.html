WealthBoard

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 9, 2026

Overview

WealthBoard ("the App") is an investment portfolio tracker for Android, developed by Tristan David. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use the App.

Short version: WealthBoard does not collect or store your personal financial data on any server. Your portfolio data stays on your device. The App displays ads via Google AdMob, which may collect certain device identifiers.

Information We Collect

The App itself does not collect, transmit, or store any personally identifiable information on external servers. Specifically:

  • Portfolio data you enter (stocks, ETFs, crypto, dividends) is stored locally on your device only
  • We do not create user accounts or require registration
  • We do not collect your name, email address, or financial account credentials
  • We do not transmit your investment data to any third party

Advertising — Google AdMob

WealthBoard uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect the following information to serve relevant ads:

  • Device identifiers (Advertising ID)
  • IP address and approximate location
  • Device type, OS version, and app usage data
  • Ad interaction data (views, clicks)

This data is collected and processed by Google LLC under their own privacy policy. You can review Google's data practices and opt out of personalized advertising at policies.google.com/privacy.

To opt out of interest-based ads on Android, go to Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID.

Market Data

The App may request publicly available market price data (stock quotes, exchange rates) from third-party financial data providers. These requests contain no personal information — only the ticker symbols you have added to your portfolio.

Data Storage & Security

Your portfolio information is stored exclusively in local app storage on your Android device. It is subject to Android's built-in app sandboxing protections. We do not operate servers that store your financial data, and no backup is transmitted over the internet by the App itself.

If you uninstall the App, all locally stored portfolio data is deleted from your device.

Children's Privacy

WealthBoard is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

Your Choices

  • Ad personalization: You can reset or delete your Android Advertising ID in device settings
  • Delete your data: Uninstalling the App removes all locally stored portfolio data
  • Third-party opt-outs: Visit Google's Ad Settings to manage personalized advertising preferences

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the effective date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
