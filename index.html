What it does

WealthBoard keeps your holdings, transactions and dividends in one place and shows them against live market data — total value and day change, performance over any range, dividend income by month and by holding, allocation across accounts, and the tax treatment of each account.

Everything is entered by you. WealthBoard does not connect to your brokerage, cannot place trades, and is not investment advice.
