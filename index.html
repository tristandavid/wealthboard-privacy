Frequently asked questions

How do I back up or restore my portfolio?

Open Menu → Backup & Restore. You can export a backup file at any time, or sign in with Apple or Google to enable automatic cloud sync across your devices.

Are my brokerage accounts connected automatically?

No. WealthBoard is not a brokerage and does not connect to your bank or trading accounts. You add holdings yourself or import the CSV files your broker lets you download, and WealthBoard handles tracking, dividend forecasting and reporting from there.

How does dividend forecasting work?

Projected income is built from each holding's own payment history and cadence, not a generic estimate — so a fund's forecast reflects how that specific fund actually pays.

What does WealthBoard Premium include?

Premium unlocks every analysis card (rebalancing planner, multi-year income forecast, dividend raises and cuts, historical yield, benchmark comparison and risk metrics), price and ex-dividend alerts, cloud backup and multi-device sync, PDF and CSV exports, and unlimited accounts. Free users can unlock all of the analysis for 30 minutes by watching a short, optional ad. Premium has no ads.

How do I use WealthBoard on the web?

Subscribe to Premium + Web in the app while signed in (Menu → Sign In / Register). Then go to app.wealthboardapp.com and sign in with the same account, using email and password, Google, or Apple. Your portfolio syncs over automatically. Existing Premium subscribers can switch to Premium + Web from the Premium screen, and the unused part of their current plan is credited.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID or Google Play account settings, not inside the app. Cancelling stops the next renewal; Premium stays active until the end of the period you've already paid for.

How do I delete my account or data?

Email support and we'll process the request. See our Privacy Policy for details on what's stored and how it's handled.
