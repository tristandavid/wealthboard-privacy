Frequently asked questions

How do I back up or restore my portfolio?

Open Menu → Backup & Restore. You can export a backup file at any time, or sign in with Apple or Google to enable automatic cloud sync across your devices.

Are my brokerage accounts connected automatically?

No. WealthBoard is not a brokerage and does not connect to your bank or trading accounts. You add your holdings manually, and WealthBoard handles tracking, dividend forecasting, and reporting from there.

How does dividend forecasting work?

Projected income is built from each holding's own payment history and cadence, not a generic estimate — so a fund's forecast reflects how that specific fund actually pays.

What does WealthBoard Premium include?

Premium adds cloud backup and multi-device sync and PDF report generation. Everything else in the app is available on the free tier.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID or Google Play account settings, not inside the app. Cancelling stops the next renewal; Premium stays active until the end of the period you've already paid for.

How do I delete my account or data?

Email support and we'll process the request. See our Privacy Policy for details on what's stored and how it's handled.
